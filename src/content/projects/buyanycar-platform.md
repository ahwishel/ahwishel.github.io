# BuyAnyCar Platform APIs

Part of my work at SellAnyCar.com was building and scaling the APIs and SDKs that integrate AI models and agentic systems into the BuyAnyCar social platform. These power automated customer touchpoints across the app.

On the messaging side I architected and scaled the APIs that support real-time chat and WhatsApp integration across multiple applications, so buyers and sellers can actually talk to each other. I also enhanced the legacy CRM systems and optimized database performance by 30% to keep inspectors and dealers working at speed.

The result is a platform where AI handles the routine load, chat works in real time, and the underlying systems stay fast under it all.
